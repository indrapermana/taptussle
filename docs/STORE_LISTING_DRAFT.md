# Tap Tussle store listing draft

## Listing copy

**App name:** Tap Tussle

**Subtitle / short description:** 18 mini-games for friends and family on one device.

**Promotional text:** Pick a quick game, share the screen, and play together—offline, with no account, ads, or in-app purchases.

**Full description:**

Tap Tussle is a colorful collection of 18 mini-games made for quick play on one
phone or tablet. Play solo, challenge a bot, or share the device with up to four
players.

Race in Lane Dash, react first in Reaction Duel, play familiar board games such
as Ludo, Chess, Checkers, and Snakes & Ladders, or relax with puzzles including
Sudoku, Water Sort, Solitaire, and Nuts & Bolts. Large controls, clear turn
feedback, friendly artwork, and adjustable sound and motion settings help younger
players understand what to do.

- 18 games in one app
- Solo, bot, and same-device multiplayer modes
- Supports one to four players, depending on the game
- Three bot difficulty levels where supported
- Works fully offline after installation
- No account, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases
- Preferences and records stay on the device
