This file consolidates the attribution records that must be retained with the
release archive.

- Tap Tussle launcher icon and splash artwork were supplied by the project owner
  on 24 September 2026 and generated with OpenAI ChatGPT at the owner's direction.
- The 18 text-free catalog tiles were supplied by the project owner on

Source-level details are retained in `assets/branding/ATTRIBUTION.md`,
`assets/game_logos/ATTRIBUTION.md`, and the game asset directories.
